Explore the methodology ↗
  1. Signed boundary

    Scope and authorise

    Agree on targets, exclusions, and stop conditions in writing. A named signatory clears the work before it begins.

  2. Controlled simulation

    Rehearse the assault

    Run the simulation from a monitored jump host. Each action is timestamped against the authorised scope.

  3. Replayable evidence

    Prove and replay

    Reproduce the intrusion path with the people who will fix it. Hash the evidence rather than carrying client data away.

  4. Verified retest

    Rebuild and retest

    Hand over a prioritised remediation queue, then use the retest window to confirm which fixes hold.

Our operating rule
