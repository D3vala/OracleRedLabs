Methodology and Armory

Oracle Red Labs exists because most organisations know their vulnerability counts and none of them know their intrusion paths. We are in the business of producing the second number.

BOUNDARY / OBSERVED

Who We Are

Operators first, consultants second

The firm was founded by four engineers who spent a decade on the defensive side of network operations and incident response. That background shapes how we work: we write the report we always wished we had received at 03:00, we keep reproduction steps short enough to follow under pressure, and we assume your team is competent rather than negligent.

Our engagements are simulations, not scans. A scan tells you which versions of software you run; a simulation tells you which of those facts an adversary would combine, in what order, and at what cost to you. We work from a signed scope, log every action against that scope, and consider a test failed if we cannot replay it for your engineers afterwards.

Method is deliberately boring and repeatable. Reconnaissance is documented, hypotheses are written down before they are tested, and anything that cannot be reproduced gets dropped from the report. If we cannot show it twice, we do not claim it.

Working Principles

Rules we hold ourselves to

Authorisation first
No packet leaves our network until a named signatory has approved the scope in writing.
Stoppable at any time
Your duty officer can halt an engagement with one phone call, and we log the halt.
Reversibility
Anything we change, we can change back. Destructive objectives are refused outright.
Evidence, not exfiltration
Proof objects are hashed where they sit. Customer data is never copied out.
