How It Runs

Four moves, no surprises

The same sequence every time: agree the rules, rehearse the attack, prove it happened, then rebuild.

  1. Move 01

    Scope and authorise

    We write the target list, the excluded systems and the stop conditions together. Nothing starts until a named signatory approves the scope.

  2. Move 02

    Rehearse the assault

    Operators run the simulation from a monitored jump host. Every action is timestamped against the authorisation ledger.

  3. Move 03

    Prove and replay

    Findings are reproduced in front of your team, in a session you can record, with the evidence hashed rather than exfiltrated.

  4. Move 04

    Rebuild and retest

    You get a prioritised remediation queue; a retest window closes the loop and confirms the fix actually holds.
