1. To Terrify and Validate: To demonstrate, through interactive demos and case studies, that
traditional defenses are insufficient—and that our attackers are smarter than yours.
2. To Generate Qualified Leads: Convert visiting CISOs, Security Architects, and DevOps leads into
booking a free "Attack Surface Triage" or a live demo of the ORACLE AI.

A dedicated resource section hosts a filterable grid of anonymized case studies, technical
