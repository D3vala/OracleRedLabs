Who We Serve

Four industries where a successful intrusion is measured in downtime, regulatory exposure and patient or customer harm. Each has a different threat model, so each engagement starts from a different question.

Industry Threat Models

Where simulated assault pays for itself

Financial Services

Your threat model is monetisation: payment-flow abuse, credential stuffing against customer portals, and insiders with settlement authority. Attackers care about minutes of access, not months of stealth.

  • Payment and transfer-flow manipulation
  • Customer-facing authentication bypass
  • Segregation-of-duties gaps in back-office tooling

Typical line: ORACLE AI plus a PHANTASM insider window.

Healthcare and Biotech

Clinical availability and research integrity come first. We treat medical devices and lab instrumentation as excluded by default and rehearse the ransomware scenario your continuity plan assumes away.

  • Ransomware containment across clinical networks
  • Research data theft through collaboration channels
  • Third-party and vendor access into patient systems

Typical line: PHANTASM with a tabletop for clinical leads.

Enterprise SaaS

Multi-tenant isolation and identity are the whole game: a single cross-tenant read is a breach notification, not a bug. We test the gap between what your code authorises and what your data layer actually returns.

  • Tenant isolation escapes and IDOR chains
  • Federated identity and token-scope abuse
  • CI/CD and artifact provenance for shipped code

Typical line: CHAINBREAK plus a targeted ORACLE AI window.

Critical Infrastructure

Availability is safety. We model pre-positioning by a patient adversary, the jump from corporate IT into operational technology, and how far a compromised vendor maintenance path can reach.

  • IT-to-OT boundary crossings
  • Remote maintenance and vendor jump hosts
  • Detectability of long-dwell pre-positioning

Typical line: PHANTASM with read-only objectives.

Honest Fit

When we are the wrong choice

We are a poor fit if

  • You need a compliance tick-box rather than a demonstrated intrusion path
  • Nobody with authority can sign the authorisation or stop the test
  • You want a scan report with no human analysis attached to it

We are a strong fit if

  • You can name the crown-jewel system we must not reach
  • Your detection team wants to be tested on the record
  • You will fix the top three findings before the retest window closes

Tell us which of the four you are

We will send the threat model we would start from, the parts we would exclude, and an indicative timeline.
