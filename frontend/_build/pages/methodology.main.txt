        <div class="prose-grid">
          <div>
            <p class="eyebrow">Who We Are</p>
            <h2 id="about-heading">Operators first, consultants second</h2>

            <p>
              The firm was founded by four engineers who spent a decade on the
              defensive side of network operations and incident response. That
              background shapes how we work: we write the report we always wished
              we had received at 03:00, we keep reproduction steps short enough to
              follow under pressure, and we assume your team is competent rather
              than negligent.
            </p>

            <p>
              Our engagements are simulations, not scans. A scan tells you which
              versions of software you run; a simulation tells you which of those
              facts an adversary would combine, in what order, and at what cost to
              you. We work from a signed scope, log every action against that
              scope, and consider a test failed if we cannot replay it for your
              engineers afterwards.
            </p>

            <p>
              Method is deliberately boring and repeatable. Reconnaissance is
              documented, hypotheses are written down before they are tested, and
              anything that cannot be reproduced gets dropped from the report. If
              we cannot show it twice, we do not claim it.
            </p>
          </div>

          <aside class="card" aria-labelledby="principles-heading">
            <span class="card__label">Working Principles</span>
            <h3 id="principles-heading">Rules we hold ourselves to</h3>
            <dl class="info-list">
              <dt>Authorisation first</dt>
              <dd>No packet leaves our network until a named signatory has approved the scope in writing.</dd>
              <dt>Stoppable at any time</dt>
              <dd>Your duty officer can halt an engagement with one phone call, and we log the halt.</dd>
              <dt>Reversibility</dt>
              <dd>Anything we change, we can change back. Destructive objectives are refused outright.</dd>
              <dt>Evidence, not exfiltration</dt>
              <dd>Proof objects are hashed where they sit. Customer data is never copied out.</dd>
            </dl>
          </aside>
        </div>
